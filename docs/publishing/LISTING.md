Villager Reroller

Villager Reroller adds a book button to the villager trade screen. The button opens a list of every offer that villager's profession can roll at the current level, with the vanilla minimum and maximum emerald price. Pick one or more trades, set the most you want to pay, and press Start.

By default, Start breaks the job-site block and places it again until one of the chosen offers appears at or under that price. That is the same break-and-replace a player can do by hand. The mod stops when a match is found, and it refuses a villager who has already been traded with. Names that do not fit the row scroll, and the price stays in its own column.

Use it when you want to see the full price range before you commit to a trade, and when you would rather not break the lectern yourself over and over.

Requirements for Minecraft 1.21.11:

- Fabric Loader
- Fabric API. Required. The mod uses it for keybinds and screen events.
- Cloth Config. Required. The settings screen (enabled switch, attempt cap, and panic stop) is built with it, and the mod will not load without it.
- Mod Menu. Required. It is how you open that settings screen, and the mod will not load without it. The reroll key can also be changed in the vanilla Controls menu.

Trade Cycling is optional. If Max Henkel's Trade Cycling mod is installed, the panel can switch the reroll to that mod's cycle action and leave the job block in place. Villager Reroller does not include Trade Cycling, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by Trade Cycling or its author.

The default reroll needs the job-site block and a suitable tool in your inventory, and the villager has to be standing next to that block. A villager with trade experience, a level above novice, or a used offer is refused.

Multiplayer

On a server, Villager Reroller automates the vanilla break-and-replace a player can do by hand. It gives no advantage over other players. Check a server's rules before using it there.
